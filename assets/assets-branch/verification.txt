5. why it is a route handler and not a next.config rewrite
==============================================================
raw.githubusercontent's own headers for the same file:
  HTTP/2 200 
  content-security-policy: default-src 'none'; style-src 'unsafe-inline'; sandbox
  content-type: application/octet-stream
  x-content-type-options: nosniff

  An external rewrite passes those through verbatim (measured locally against
  next start), and a headers() entry on /curriculum.pdf does not override them.
  application/octet-stream + nosniff + sandbox CSP makes a browser download the
  resume as an unnamed blob instead of opening it.
